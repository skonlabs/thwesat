# ThweSat — One Pager

---

## The Problem

Myanmar's job market is fragmented and opaque. Talented professionals struggle to stand out in a system that still runs largely on word-of-mouth and informal connections. Employers waste weeks sifting through unqualified applicants. Experienced professionals who could mentor the next generation have no structured way to do so. And the tools that exist — job boards, LinkedIn, freelance platforms — were built for markets that look nothing like Myanmar's, offered only in English, and priced for Western purchasing power.

---

## What ThweSat Is

ThweSat is Myanmar's all-in-one career platform — a bilingual (English + Myanmar) ecosystem where job seekers find work, professionals earn by mentoring, and employers discover talent. It is not a job board. It is a career operating system.

At its core, ThweSat connects three communities: job seekers who want to get hired and grow, mentors who want to monetize their experience and give back, and employers who need to hire and retain quality talent. Each group gets a purpose-built experience on a single, deeply integrated platform — supported by AI tools, real-time messaging, a moderated community, and a transparent payment system built for local realities.

For job seekers, ThweSat provides AI-powered tools to build profiles, generate cover letters, and identify skill gaps — tools previously available only to those who could afford expensive career coaches. Premium members get unlimited access to these tools and to mentors, plus priority visibility with employers.

For mentors, ThweSat is a monetization engine. They set their own rates, publish availability, accept bookings, and receive 85% of session fees directly. Reviews are only unlocked after real completed sessions, keeping the marketplace honest.

For employers, ThweSat replaces the chaos of inbound applications with a structured pipeline — job posting, applicant tracking, talent search, placement fee management — all in one dashboard, at a fraction of the cost of traditional recruitment agencies.

---

## Why Now

Myanmar's digital workforce is growing rapidly. Smartphone penetration is high, mobile-first usage dominates, and a generation of professionals is entering the market hungry for modern tools. Yet no platform has been built specifically for this context — bilingual, locally priced, mobile-optimized, and culturally appropriate. ThweSat is built from the ground up for Myanmar, not adapted from elsewhere.

---

## Target Market

**Primary:** Mid-career professionals (25–40) in Myanmar seeking better employment opportunities or career growth — particularly those in tech, finance, marketing, and operations who are underserved by traditional recruitment.

**Secondary:** Experienced professionals (35–55) with domain expertise ready to monetize their knowledge as mentors — consultants, former executives, senior practitioners.

**Tertiary:** SMEs, startups, and growing companies in Myanmar that need efficient hiring tools but cannot afford full-service recruitment agencies.

---

## Revenue Model

ThweSat runs a multi-stream revenue model designed for high retention across all user types.

**Subscriptions (Job Seekers):** Premium plans unlock AI career tools, unlimited mentor connections, and elevated employer visibility. Priced accessibly for the Myanmar market with monthly and annual tiers.

**Subscriptions (Employers):** Tiered plans based on the number of active job postings and access to advanced talent search. Higher tiers unlock bulk applicant exports, featured job placement, and dedicated support.

**Mentor Sessions:** ThweSat takes a 15% platform fee on every mentor booking. As session volume scales, this becomes a significant recurring revenue stream with zero marginal cost to the platform.

**Placement Fees:** When a job seeker placed through the platform is hired, employers pay a placement fee — a success-based revenue layer that aligns ThweSat's incentives with actual outcomes.

---

## Go-to-Market

**Phase 1 — Community First.** ThweSat launches with a curated cohort of respected mentors and power-user job seekers. These early participants seed the community, generate authentic reviews, and create social proof. Content from the community — guides, posts, success stories — drives organic search and word-of-mouth growth.

**Phase 2 — Employer Activation.** With a credible talent pool established, ThweSat approaches employers directly through partnerships with industry associations, startup networks, and HR communities. The pitch is simple: better candidates, better tools, lower cost than agencies.

**Phase 3 — Network Effects.** As the mentor pool deepens, job seekers have more reasons to stay. As the talent pool grows, employers have more reasons to post. As employers post more jobs, job seekers have more reasons to join. ThweSat is designed to become more valuable as it grows, not less.

**Channels:** Telegram (already a dominant communication tool in Myanmar), targeted social media, referral incentives built into the platform, and partnerships with universities and professional associations.

The platform is live, the product is full-featured, and the market is ready. The question is not whether this platform will exist. The question is who builds it first.

ThweSat is that platform.
